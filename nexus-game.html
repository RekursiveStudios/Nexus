HOW TO PLAY

A secret 5-letter word is hidden. Drag each reel up or down (or tap the letter to type it), then press ENTER.

After each guess, hints appear above each reel — arrows point toward where the target letter sits on the reel:

● exact letter
v within 4 — target is later (below on reel)
^ within 4 — target is earlier (above on reel)
v
v more than 4 later
^
^ more than 4 earlier

Your previous guess letter stays highlighted on the reel in red so you can see where the arrows point from. You get 4 tries.
